## The problem

The platform needed to execute multiple independent AI agents while
keeping job state, logs and retries consistent.

<Callout title="Main architectural decision">

Each agent runs as an isolated worker. Redis handles short-lived
coordination, while PostgreSQL stores durable workflow state.

</Callout>
